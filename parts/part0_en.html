Preface

Welcome to A Total Beginner's Guide to openEHR Clinical Knowledge Modelling.

My name is Martin Koch. I hold a PhD in Biomedical Engineering, and since 2023, I have been working actively with openEHR as a Clinical Knowledge Modeller.

When I first stepped into the openEHR ecosystem, I found the learning curve quite steep. It is remarkably easy to "get lost in the sauce" and be overwhelmed by complex specifications, technical jargon, and code before even grasping how clinical concepts are actually represented and structured.

I wrote this guide for anyone starting completely from scratch. Rather than diving straight into code, we will focus entirely on the Clinical Knowledge Modeller's perspective. You will learn the core architecture and practical principles needed to begin designing and working with openEHR archetypes.

Whether you are coming from a clinical or an engineering background: if you are curious about Clinical Knowledge Modelling using openEHR, this guide is for you.
